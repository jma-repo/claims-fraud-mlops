What is real and what is simulated

  • Real: the pipeline, the models, MLflow tracking and registry, the online/batch feature parity, the API, the drift checks, the local LLM and its evaluation. Every number on this page was computed by the run that produced it.
  • Simulated: the claims. They are synthetic, generated with seven documented fraud mechanisms and plenty of legitimate claims that share their red flags. Providers and people are coded IDs.
  • Assumed: the business inputs (capacity, cost per file, recovery rate). They sit in one config file and are printed next to every dollar figure.
  • Hindsight: “hidden truth” checks on the newest month use labels a real carrier would not have yet; they exist to show whether an alert was right.
