# Model Card: claims-fraud-triage version 1

Generated by the pipeline from the run's own results. All data is synthetic.

## Intended use

Ranks newly reported auto claims so the SIU can choose which 20 files to open each month, and marks low-risk claims (fraud probability below 0.03) for fast-track payment. It is not used for denying claims, pricing, underwriting or deciding coverage, and a score is never evidence of fraud.

## Training data

Training months 2024-09 to 2026-02 (12,160 claims, fraud rate 5.7%). Validation months 2026-03 to 2026-05 were used for early stopping, calibration and choosing between candidates. Test months 2026-06 to 2026-08 (2,042 claims) were used once, for the results below. The split is by time, never random, because the model has to predict next month.

## Features the model uses

- What happened: loss type, province, amounts (total, injury reserve, repair), repair-to-value ratio, total loss, hour of loss, occupants and injured, soft-tissue only, police report, witnesses, towing distance, storage days and fees.
- Who is claiming: policy age at the time of loss, days between loss and report, prior claims in three years, whether the claimant is represented.
- Network: how often the phone number and address appeared on claims in the past year, body shop, clinic and tow operator volumes, the body shop's average repair-to-value ratio, the tow operator's average storage days, and repeated pairings of the same representative and clinic.
- Narrative: a text model's probability that the adjuster notes resemble confirmed fraud files (place names removed).

## Features deliberately excluded

- claimant_age: protected ground under human-rights law; fraud triage must not rank people by age.
- city: location is a proxy for neighbourhood and, through it, for protected characteristics.
- narrative place names: scrubbed from adjuster notes before the text model sees them, for the same reason.
- provider IDs: used only through behaviour (volumes, ratios, pairings), never as an identity the model memorises.

The model does not use the claimant's age, sex, ethnicity, language or any other protected ground.

## Performance on the held-out test months

The champion is a lightgbm model. On the test months it reached ROC-AUC 0.878 and PR-AUC 0.628, against 0.696 and 0.280 for the red-flag scorecard used today.

With the SIU's real capacity of 20 files a month, the model's queue caught 15.7 frauds a month (78% of opened files) and 79% of fraudulent dollars, for a net saving of $543,847 a month. The scorecard's queue caught 12.7 frauds (63% of files) for $468,865 a month, and picking files at random would save $-2,925. The model also opened 4.3 files a month on honest customers, against 7.3 for the scorecard.

## Decision rule

Claims are ranked by expected net saving: probability x claimed amount x recovery rate (70%) minus the investigation cost ($1,200). Claims with a negative expected saving are not opened. Claims with a probability of 0.25 or more that do not make the queue go to an adjuster's red-flag review.

## What drives the score

Average absolute contribution to the log-odds on the test months (higher means more influential):

- Narrative resembles confirmed fraud notes: 0.685
- Body shop's average repair-to-value ratio: 0.325
- Repair estimate vs vehicle value: 0.303
- Total claimed: 0.256
- Loss type: 0.255
- Body shop claim volume: 0.177
- Policy age: 0.176
- Repair estimate: 0.163
- Vehicle value: 0.093
- Number injured: 0.075

## Known limitations

- The model only recognises fraud that looks like fraud it was trained on. A new scheme is invisible to it until investigators label examples; the pattern monitor exists to catch exactly that gap.
- In a real deployment, labels exist only for claims that were investigated, so the training data over-represents what investigators already suspected.
- Calibration was fitted on three months with few confirmed frauds; probabilities above 0.5 are less precise than the ranking.
- Soft fraud (padding an honest loss) is common and mostly undetectable from these features.
